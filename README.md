Free tools for manga and comic artists, made by Jaime Maia, illustrator, Designer and Comic Artist from Brazil.

They run right in your browser, work offline once loaded, and need no installation or sign-up. The interface is available in English and Portuguese, and the tools also work on iPad.

👤 Head Generator

A tool for studying and constructing heads, with adjustable angles, proportions and lighting.

Six construction methods: Loomis, Box, Atari Manga, Fifths, Reilly and Bridgman
Three styles: Classic, Manga and Realistic
Male, female and child head presets
Solid head with facial planes and seven light presets
Guides that follow the form of the face
Your own drawing behind the model as a reference
Export as PNG, SVG, a flexible model sheet (up to nine views) and a 3D model (GLB)

👉 Open the Head Generator

💥 Manga FX Generator

A fully customizable generator for manga effect lines and screentones.

18 effects: focus lines, flash rays, speed lines, rain and wind lines, cross-hatching (kakeami), halftones, pattern tones, stipple, grain and mood shapes
Up to 8 layers, each with its own color, opacity and effect area (rectangle, ellipse or hand-drawn polygon)
Print-ready: document dpi, B4/B5/A4/A5/B6 presets, effects that scale with the page, and halftones in lines per inch (60 lpi, 65 lpi…)
Zoomable preview, undo/redo and a reference image for placing effects over your art
Save your own presets and move them between devices as a file
Export as PNG (transparent or white), SVG with named layers, ZIP with one image per layer, or copy to clipboard

How to use

Click a tool's link and use it right in your browser. Each tool has a ? button with a quick guide and shortcuts.

To use a tool without internet, open it, save the page with Ctrl+S (or Cmd+S on Mac), and open the saved file whenever you like. You can also download it from the Releases page.
