Free tools for manga and comic artists, made by Jaime Maia, illustrator and Product Designer from Brazil.

They all run right in your browser, work offline once loaded, and need no installation or sign-up.

🧑‍🎨 Head Generator

A tool for studying and constructing heads, with adjustable angles, proportions and other parameters.

Six construction methods: Loomis, Box, Atari Manga, Fifths, Reilly and Bridgman
Three styles: Classic, Manga and Realistic
Male, female and child head presets
Solid head with adjustable lighting
Option to place your own drawing behind the model as a reference

👉 Open the Head Generator

💥 Effects Generator

A fully customizable generator for manga effect lines and tones.

Focus (concentration) lines and speed lines
Screentone
Export as SVG and PNG (transparent or white background)
Interface in Portuguese and English
Also works on iPad

👉 Open the Effects Generator

How to use

Click a tool's link and use it right in your browser. To use it offline, open the page, save it with Ctrl+S (or Cmd+S on Mac), and open the saved file whenever you like.
